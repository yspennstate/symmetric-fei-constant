## Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies
The paper comes out of Math Brain, an ongoing project on the use of AI systems for research mathematics, and its results were developed with the Brain and Math Brain. In November 2026, Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) and a team of Codex agents running OpenAI's GPT-6.1, all connected to the Brain, retrieved the spectral-level entropy lemma and related methods from the author's earlier work through Math Brain, derived the noise-stability estimate and the split of the entropy into level and within-level parts, proved the Bessel-function inequalities and the asymptotic restriction estimate, found the bound for the zero-level coefficient that closes the restriction step in every dimension, derived the averaging argument over successive dimensions and the sharper shift estimate, wrote and ran the interval certificates and the exhaustive computation for small dimensions, and searched the literature. Separate agents checked the averaging argument, the sharper shift estimate and the changed analytic cutoff, and recomputed the certificates with independently written code. Claude Opus 5.5 coordinated the agents, assembled the proofs, prepared the manuscript, and audited it, including the proofs, the numerical constants and the finite computations. These checks are not peer review. The author directed the work and is responsible for the content of the paper and for decisions concerning its dissemination.
